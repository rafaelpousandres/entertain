## 2. What information we collect

Entertain is designed to be minimal. We only collect what is necessary
to make the app work.

### 2.1 Information you provide

You provide the following information by entering it into the app:

- **Event information**: event names, dates, types (seated, buffet,
  other), number of guests.
- **Catalog information**: dish names, descriptions, preparation notes,
  ingredients, quantities, units.
- **Supplier configuration**: supplier category labels, supplier names
  (if you choose to record them), phone numbers, email addresses,
  preferred messaging channel.
- **Message templates**: greeting and signature text for supplier
  messages.
- **Guest lists**: for each event, the guests you add — names and, if
  you choose to record them, phone numbers, email addresses, dietary
  restrictions and whether they are attending.
- **Photos**: the photos you choose to add to events, dishes,
  ingredients and drinks, taken with the camera or picked from your
  photo library from within the app.
- **Suggestions**: the text of any suggestion you send us through the
  app's Suggestions screen.
- **Requests to the AI assistant**: the text you type when you ask the
  app to create a dish or propose a menu (see section 5.1).

This information is stored under an anonymous user account
automatically created on first launch. We do **not** ask for your
name, email address, phone number, address, age, or any other
personal identifier when you start using the app.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

- **Anonymous user identifier**: when you first open the app, a random
  identifier is generated to associate your data with your device.
  This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any other
  personally identifiable information.
- **Technical operational data**: basic technical information needed
  to keep the app functioning (database connection metadata, request
  timestamps). No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers,
  device fingerprints, or location data are collected.

### 2.3 Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect:

- Your real name, postal address, or government-issued identifiers.
- Your phone contacts list. If you use "Pick from contacts", only the
  one contact you pick is read, to fill in a supplier or a guest.
- Your photos, files, or any content stored on your device, other than
  the specific photos you choose to add to the app.
- Your precise or approximate location.
- Browsing history, app usage analytics, or behavioural tracking.
- Advertising identifiers.
- Microphone recordings, or camera images other than the photos you
  take from within the app to add to it.

## 3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on servers operated by **Supabase**
(https://supabase.com), located in **Frankfurt, Germany (European
Union)**. Supabase is a third-party infrastructure provider acting as
a data processor on behalf of the data controller.

All data transmission between the app and the storage servers is
encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

The requests described in section 5.1 (AI features and stock photo
search) are processed by providers based in the **United States**, so
the content of those specific requests leaves the European Union.

## 4. How we use your data

Your data is used solely for the operation of the app:

- To display your events, catalog, and shopping lists across app
  sessions.
- To compose supplier messages from your stored data when you choose
  to send them.
- To synchronise your data between any devices where you may install
  the app under the same anonymous user identifier.
- To generate dishes and menus, and to translate catalog names, when
  you use the features described in section 5.1.

We do **not** use your data for:

- Advertising or marketing.
- Profiling, analytics, or behavioural research.
- Selling, renting, or sharing with third parties for any commercial
  purpose.
- Training machine learning models of our own.

## 5. Sharing with third parties

We do **not** share your data with any third party, except:

- **Supabase**, as the infrastructure provider that hosts the
  database and the stored photos. Supabase is contractually bound to
  handle your data only on our instructions and in compliance with
  the GDPR.
- **Anthropic** and **Pexels**, only for the specific features and
  the specific data described in section 5.1 below.
- **Legal obligations**: if required by a valid legal order (court
  order, law enforcement request) under applicable EU and Spanish law.

### 5.1 Artificial intelligence and stock photos

Some features of the app rely on external providers. The requests go
from the app to our own server, and from our server to the provider;
the provider does not receive your anonymous user identifier.

**Anthropic (artificial intelligence).** Entertain uses AI models
provided by Anthropic, PBC (United States, https://www.anthropic.com)
for three features:

- **AI dish assistant** — when you ask the app to create a dish, we
  send Anthropic the text you typed, the app language, and the names
  of the ingredients and supplier categories in your catalog (so the
  new dish can reuse them).
- **AI menu wizard** — when you ask the app to propose a menu, we
  send Anthropic the event's number of guests, format and type, the
  text and answers you entered, and the names of the dishes,
  ingredients, drinks and supplier categories in your catalog.
- **Automatic name translation** — when you create or rename a dish,
  an ingredient or a drink, its name is sent to Anthropic to be
  translated into Catalan, Spanish and English. This happens
  automatically, without a separate request from you.

We never send Anthropic your guest lists, the names, phone numbers or
email addresses of your suppliers, your photos, or your messages. (A
supplier *category* is a label such as "Fishmonger"; if you name a
category after a person or a shop, that label is among the catalog
names sent.) The text you
type into the AI assistant is sent as you wrote it, so please do not
include personal data in it.

The first time you open the AI dish assistant or the AI menu wizard,
the app explains this and asks for your agreement before anything is
sent. Anthropic handles the data it receives under its own terms and
privacy policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).

**Pexels (stock photos).** When you search for a stock photo inside
the app, the search words are sent to Pexels (https://www.pexels.com)
to obtain the results. No other data is sent. Pexels handles the data
it receives under its own privacy policy
(https://www.pexels.com/privacy-policy/).

When you choose to **send a supplier message** through the app, the
content of that message is composed on your device and sent through
your chosen channel (WhatsApp, SMS, email, share sheet, etc.). At
that point, the message and its recipient are subject to the privacy
policies of the messaging service you use. We do not retain copies of
sent messages beyond the snapshot stored within the app for your own
reference.

## 6. Your rights under the GDPR

If you are located in the European Union, you have the following
rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

- **Right of access**: to know what personal data we hold about you.
- **Right of rectification**: to correct inaccurate data.
- **Right of erasure** ("right to be forgotten"): to request the
  deletion of all your data.
- **Right to data portability**: to receive a copy of your data in a
  machine-readable format.
- **Right to object**: to object to the processing of your data.
- **Right to lodge a complaint**: with the Spanish Data Protection
  Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es) or
  your local supervisory authority.

You can exercise the right of erasure yourself, at any time, from
inside the app (see section 7.1). To exercise any of the other rights,
contact us at rafael.pous@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Because Entertain creates an anonymous account by default, we may
need additional information from you to identify your data when you
exercise these rights (for example, the anonymous user identifier
visible in the app's settings, if available).

## 7. Data retention

Your data is retained as long as you continue to use the app. When
you delete your account from inside the app, all your data is
permanently removed from our storage immediately. If you instead
request deletion by email, it is removed within 30 days of the
request.

We have no offline backup retention policy beyond the operational
backups maintained by Supabase for service continuity (retained for a
short period and used only for disaster recovery). Deleted data may
remain in those backups until they expire; it is not restored or used
for any other purpose.

### 7.1 Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all your data at any
time, directly in the app: **Settings > Privacy & data > Delete my
account and data**. No email or request is needed. The full deletion
process — what is deleted, what is retained, and the alternative by
email — is described on our dedicated page:

https://rafaelpousandres.github.io/entertain/delete-data/
